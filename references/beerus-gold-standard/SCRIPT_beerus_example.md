## Verified Canon Facts & Sources (Step 1)
- **Hakai ("1 to 0" & energy release):** *Dragon Ball Super* Manga Ch. 69 ("The Evolution of Planet Cereal") — Beerus teaches Vegeta that true Destruction doesn't shatter matter; it erases matter completely from existence ("from one to zero"), releasing a massive burst of energy even from a tiny pebble.
- **Destroyer Mindset:** *Dragon Ball Super* Manga Ch. 69 — Beerus instructs Vegeta that wielding Destruction properly requires a mind fixed solely on destruction, free of guilt, doubt, or hesitation.
- **Finger-Tap Half-Planet Feat:** *Dragon Ball Super* Episode 1 / *Battle of Gods* — Annoyed by greasy food, Beerus casually taps his finger on a dining table and cleanly obliterates half the planet.
- **Hakai Aura Nullification:** *Dragon Ball Super* Episode 125 — God of Destruction Toppo cloaks himself in purple Energy of Destruction that erases incoming ki blasts and attacks on contact.
- **Soul Erasure:** *Dragon Ball Super* Episode 59 — Beerus erases Present Zamasu (and ghosts/spirits) completely from existence, body and soul.
- **Sleep Sneeze & Whis's Temporal Do-Over:** *Dragon Ball Super* (*Battle of Gods* / Episode 27, *Resurrection 'F'*) — Beerus accidentally destroyed two suns by sneezing in his sleep, and Whis's Angel ability (*Temporal Do-Over*) rewinds time by up to three minutes to undo Beerus's worst mistakes.

## Voiceover Script v2 (187 words, target ~46–52 s @ ~3.8–4.0 words/s)

You wake up with Beerus's Destruction power, tap your coffee mug, and wipe it straight out of existence.
No shards, no smoke — just purple sparks fading to zero.
That's Hakai. It doesn't break matter; it deletes anything it touches, body and soul.
Tap a brick wall, and half of it vanishes in a razor-clean line.
Wrap yourself in purple aura, and bullets evaporate before touching your skin.
Erase one tiny pebble, and turning matter from one to zero triggers a shockwave that craters the whole street.
Wait — why did a pebble do that?
You see, Hakai unleashes violent energy every time something vanishes, and it demands a mind locked purely on destruction.
One second of guilt or panic breaks your grip, letting the purple fire eat straight back into your own hand.
Worse, Beerus once sneezed in his sleep and erased two suns, and casually tapped a table to split a planet in half.
Even he needed Whis — an Angel who rewinds time by three minutes — to undo his mistakes.
And when your finger slips on Earth, there's no Angel here to turn back the clock.
